# SEO, GEO, and AEO Audit and Strategy

## Executive Summary

Star Safety Enterprises already has strong local-service content and a functioning structured-data foundation. The biggest opportunity is to make each page more explicitly answer user questions, improve topical authority, and increase machine-readability for Google, AI answer engines, and local search.

The recommended strategy is to focus on:

- Stronger page-level intent matching for high-value local searches such as “balcony safety nets Chennai”, “pigeon netting Chennai”, “invisible grills Chennai”, and “construction safety nets Tamil Nadu”.
- Better GEO/AEO formatting with direct answers, FAQs, comparison sections, and concise summaries before deeper detail.
- Consistent entity-rich content around the brand, services, locations, and trust signals.
- Continued content expansion through topical clusters and service-area landing pages.

## 3. Recommended Content Structure for Each Page

### SEO Title
Use a pattern like:
- “Balcony Safety Nets in Chennai | Certified Installation & Quote”
- “Pigeon Safety Nets Chennai | Humane Bird Protection for Balconies”
- “Invisible Grills Chennai | Stainless Steel Balcony Safety Solution”

### Meta Description
Keep descriptions under 155 characters and include one primary keyword and one local keyword.

### Suggested H1 and H2 Structure
- H1: direct answer and keyword phrase
- H2: what the service solves
- H2: why homeowners and builders choose it
- H2: what is included
- H2: FAQs

## 4. GEO and AEO Recommendations

For every page:
- Start with a concise summary.
- Answer the main question in the first 2–3 sentences.
- Add FAQs with complete answers.
- Use entity-rich phrases such as “balcony safety net installation”, “coastal-grade stainless steel anchors”, and “UV-stabilized HDPE mesh”.
- Provide comparison tables where readers are deciding between options.
- Keep content human-first and easy to scan.

## 9. Technical SEO Recommendations

- Keep metadata consistent across desktop and mobile.
- Ensure canonical tags are correct and unique by page.
- Add strong heading hierarchy and descriptive alt text.
- Improve page speed through compressed hero images and lazy loading.
- Add security headers such as X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, and Strict-Transport-Security.
- Maintain XML sitemaps and keep them updated as new pages are published.
